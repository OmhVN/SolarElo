[CENTER][SIZE=5][B]🛡️ Intelligent Anti-Farm & Security[/B][/SIZE][/CENTER]

Security is at the heart of SolarElo. Our anti-exploit algorithms detect and neutralize win-trading and alt-farming instantly:

[LIST]
[*][B]IP & Subnet Check[/B] — Blocks ELO changes when players defeat alts sharing the same IP or /24 subnet.
[*][B]AFK Detection[/B] — Blocks ELO gains if the victim hasn't moved or fought back recently.
[*][B]Diminished Kill Returns[/B] — ELO gain is halved for repeated kills on the same player within a rolling hour.
[*][B]Spawn Camping Protection[/B] — Restricts ELO gains near spawn points and shortly after resurrection.
[*][B]ELO Difference Capping[/B] — Configurable threshold prevents high-ranked veterans from farming new players.
[/LIST]
